What this topic is

Guy and a Haredi caller work through why the wider draft question is so much harder: an enclave with its own education and its own systems, growing exponentially, and a state that has to integrate it slowly enough to be humane and fast enough to avoid collapse. Why Ben-Gvir Is Not Israel's Donald Trump
What this topic is

Guy says he probably agrees with what Ben-Gvir wrote, and that it is still the wrong thing for a security minister to publish without telling the foreign office. Remove him and the votes redistribute, the voice survives, and only the damage abroad disappears.
