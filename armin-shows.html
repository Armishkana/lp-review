📋 What to do

Using everything on a clip? Press Use everything on this clip - that answers all seven questions at once. Otherwise answer them one at a time. It all saves as you go, and you never have to finish the page.

The full instructions - covers, titles, subtitles, what to write
  1. You do not have to get through all of them. Armin decided this on 13 August, right after you said the dashboard was overwhelming because of how many videos there were to watch. His words: “you don’t have to review all of them if it’s overwhelming. Just review as many as you can.” That still stands. Start at the top, stop when you want to stop, and nothing below where you stopped is owed by you.
  2. If you are using everything on a clip, press one button. Use everything on this clip answers all seven questions at once - the video, all three covers, all three titles. Most clips should be one press and nothing else.
  3. The video first. Press Watch, then answer Am I using this video?
    • Not using it means the whole clip is out - the covers and the titles go with it, and you do not have to answer anything else on that clip. Just write one line saying why, so the next batch is better.
    • Using it opens the covers and the titles below.
  4. Each cover, one at a time: are you using it? You are not picking one winner. Three covers always go up and YouTube tests them against each other, so whatever you do not take from us, you make yourself:
    • Using all 3 of ours - you make none.
    • Using 2 of ours - you make 1 of your own, and the three compete.
    • Using 1 of ours - you make 2 of your own.
    • Using none of ours - you make all 3 yourself.
    The page does this arithmetic for you and says it under each clip, so you can always see what you have told us.
  5. Each title, the same way, and separately from its cover. A cover can be good under a title that is wrong, and a title can be right over a cover you do not like - so they are two answers, not one. Ask: does the video actually say the thing this title says?
    • Title says secret, hidden, or behind their back - did anyone actually say it was secret? If not, do not use it.
    • Title says something big like civil war, coup, or invasion - did they actually say those words?
    • Title names a place or a person, like Europe or Trump - is the video really about them?
    • Is the title about the main thing in the video, or a small part near the end? It should be the main thing.
    You never have to write a better one. Press Not using it and, if you can, say in a few words what was wrong. Titles work the same as covers: whatever you do not take, you write yourself.
  6. Tap any cover to see it full size. To save one, press Save this cover - if your browser does not start the download, right-click that same button and choose Save link as. Either way the file is already named after the video's title, so you never have to rename it. Copy title gives you the matching title text.
  7. If a clip has a Subtitles box, upload that file too. Right-click Save subtitles, choose Save link as, then on YouTube go to Subtitles, ADD LANGUAGE, English, Upload file, With timing. It takes about 20 seconds and it is worth it: our subtitles spell the Iranian names correctly and YouTube's automatic ones do not, and those names are what people search for. Not every clip will have one - only the longer ones to start with.
  8. When you are done, press Copy my answers in the bar at the bottom. It gives you one block of text - send that to Armin. One message for everything, not one per clip. That button is for the videos, covers and titles. The separate pill in the bottom-right corner, Write feedback, is for problems with this page itself - they go to two different places on purpose.

Blunt is more useful than polite. When you do not use something, one line about why is worth more to us than anything else on this page - it is the only way the next batch gets better. "Bad" on its own does not tell us what to change.

A clip you have not answered is not the same as a clip you approved. Anything you leave blank is reported as not answered, so nothing gets published on the assumption that your silence meant yes.

Each stream below has its own coloured dot, and the feedback button under it carries the same dot, so you can always tell which stream a button belongs to.
