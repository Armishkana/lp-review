📋 What to do

  1. Press Watch, then say whether the cut is good or bad, and add a note if something is wrong.
  2. Check the title against the video. You do not need to know anything about the topic to do this. While you watch, just ask: does the video actually say the thing the title says?
    • Title says secret, hidden, or behind their back - did anyone actually say it was secret? If not, the title is wrong.
    • Title says something big like civil war, coup, or invasion - did they actually say those words? If not, the title is wrong.
    • Title names a place or a person, like Europe or Trump - is the video really about them? If they are barely mentioned, the title is wrong.
    • Is the title about the main thing in the video, or about a small part near the end? It should be the main thing.
    If any answer is no, press Title does not match the video and write what is wrong in your own words. You do not have to fix it or think of a better title - just say what does not match.
  3. All three covers get published - YouTube tests them against each other, so you are not choosing one. Only press Report a problem on a cover if something is actually wrong with it (wrong face, text over someone, makes no sense).
  4. Tap any cover to see it full size. To save one, press Save this cover - if your browser does not start the download, right-click that same button and choose Save link as. Either way the file is already named after the video's title, so you never have to rename it. Copy title gives you the matching title text.
  5. If a clip has a Subtitles box, upload that file too. Right-click Save subtitles, choose Save link as, then on YouTube go to Subtitles, ADD LANGUAGE, English, Upload file, With timing. It takes about 20 seconds and it is worth it: our subtitles spell the Iranian names correctly and YouTube's automatic ones do not, and those names are what people search for. Not every clip will have one - only the longer ones to start with.
  6. When you have gone through them all, press Submit all feedback at the bottom. It gives you one block of text - send that to Armin. One message for everything, not one per clip.

Blunt is more useful than polite. If a cut or a cover is bad, say why - "bad" on its own does not tell us what to change.

Each stream below has its own coloured dot, and the feedback button under it carries the same dot, so you can always tell which stream a button belongs to.
